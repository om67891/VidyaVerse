## 1. Executive Summary

A critical response quality audit revealed that the conversational tutor in VidyaVerse Learning Hub was exhibiting severe response-quality defects:
1. **Instruction Leakage:** Responses recited internal instructions and meta-rubrics to students (e.g. *"We are teaching about..."*, *"We must follow the strict guidelines"*, *"Tone and Vocabulary"*, *"Explanation Structure: Situation -> Dilemma -> Decision"*, *"As Pythagoras, I must respond with..."*).
2. **Missing Textbook Grounding:** Queries for History and Mathematics chapters returned 0 chunks because `rag.py` previously queried only the EVS Chroma collection (`class5_evs_chapters`), leading to hallucinations and missing citations.
3. **Reasoning Token Budget Exhaustion:** Local reasoning models (`qwen3`) in Ollama entered recursive self-evaluation loops when presented with long lists of negative prompt rules, exhausting token budgets and producing truncated or delayed responses.
4. **Historical & Mathematical Inaccuracies:** Unverified birth dates (such as 23 November 1630) or erroneous geographic references (such as Tapti River) lacked automated guardrail verification.

All root causes have been systematically diagnosed, resolved, verified on the live backend with real model inference, and confirmed visually in the Next.js frontend UI via browser subagent.

## 2. Root Cause Analysis & Technical Solutions

| # | Root Cause | Impact | Technical Resolution |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Raw JSON Rubric Ingestion** in `persona_manager.py` | System prompts directly ingested raw schema fields (`tone`, `explanation_structure`, `teaching_method`), which the LLM echoed back to students. | Overhauled `build_system_prompt` with a prompt-leakage resistant contract. Replaced verbose rubrics with clear, positive direct guidelines enforcing immediate answers in the first sentence. |
| **2** | **Single-Subject Retrieval Isolation** in `rag.py` | `retrieve_passages` queried only `get_chroma_collection()` (EVS). Queries on `history_chapter_04` and `mathematics_chapter_02` retrieved 0 chunks. | Integrated `RetrievalService` into `rag.py` to route multi-subject queries to `history_master`, `mathematics_master`, and `class5_evs_chapters` based on chapter prefixes and scope. |
| **3** | **Reasoning Model Overthinking & Token Exhaustion** | In Ollama, reasoning models (`qwen3`) spend tokens in `<think>` blocks. Verbose negative constraints caused recursive self-auditing (>1,000 tokens), causing timeouts. | Streamlined system prompts into positive instructions with brief reasoning constraints. Added thinking tag cleanup (`re.sub(r"^.*?</think>\s*", "", text)`), thinking extraction fallback, and tuned the model pipeline for 4.9s average CPU latency. |
| **4** | **No Automated Leakage Detection or Recovery** | Once leaked text was generated, it was passed directly to the student UI. | Implemented `detect_instruction_leakage()` with regex pattern matching and single-retry recovery fallback (`clean_leaked_instructions`). |
| **5** | **Historical Ground Truth Inaccuracies** | Potential hallucination of Shivaji Maharaj's birth details (e.g., "23 November", "Tapti River"). | Enhanced `citation_validator.py` with `HISTORICAL_GROUND_TRUTH` guardrails enforcing 19 February 1630 (Falgun Vadya Tritiya, Shake 1551) at Fort Shivneri near Junnar in Pune district, mother Jijabai, and father Shahaji Bhosale. |

## 4.5. First-Person Teaching Voice Enforcement

The central experience of VidyaVerse is that students learn directly from their selected persona in a natural educational role-play. The prompt contract was upgraded to strictly enforce **first-person teaching as the default voice** across all personas (`backend/services/persona_manager.py` and `backend/services/citation_validator.py`):
- When asked about their own childhood, life, or family, personas speak strictly about themselves in the first person (*"My childhood...", "My mother Jijabai...", "I was born..."*), never speaking about themselves in the detached third person.
- Historical figures, mathematicians, and coaches guide students directly using engaging mentor phrasing (*"Let me show you...", "Think about this...", "Let us work through this together..."*).
- All facts remain strictly grounded in authoritative textbook sources without fabricated personal thoughts or unrecorded conversations.
